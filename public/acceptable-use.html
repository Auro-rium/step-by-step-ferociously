SERVICE RULES

Acceptable Use Policy

Use FINISH for real learning, not payment bypasses, credential sharing, scraping, malicious submissions, or the usual inventive nonsense that makes access controls necessary.

Effective: 2 August 2026Applies to all accounts
Contents1. Purpose2. Accounts3. Payments4. Security5. Content6. Academic integrity7. Automation8. Conduct9. Enforcement10. Reporting

1. Purpose

This policy protects learners, educators, service providers, and FINISH infrastructure. It applies to public pages, accounts, paid learning routes, assessments, projects, support channels, and payment interactions.

2. Account rules

You must not:

  • Sell, rent, transfer, or share an account at scale.
  • Use another person's account without permission.
  • Create accounts using false identity information for fraud or evasion.
  • Evade a suspension by repeatedly creating replacement accounts.
  • Expose session tokens, passwords, or access links publicly.

3. Payment and access abuse

  • Do not bypass checkout, forge payment events, manipulate order identifiers, or attempt to grant yourself access.
  • Do not use stolen payment credentials or knowingly participate in unauthorized payments.
  • Do not submit false non-delivery, unauthorized-payment, or chargeback claims after using the purchased product.
  • Do not exploit refunds by consuming substantial course content and then misrepresenting usage.
  • Do not ask support to accept screenshots, manual references, crypto, bank transfers, or other unsupported methods in place of PayPal verification.

4. Security and service integrity

You must not probe, scan, exploit, or interfere with FINISH or its providers without written authorization. Prohibited conduct includes:

  • Attempting to bypass authentication, row-level permissions, admin controls, lesson locks, quiz locks, or rate limits.
  • Introducing malware, destructive code, credential theft, or malicious links.
  • Launching denial-of-service attacks or intentionally exhausting resources.
  • Reverse engineering private APIs for abuse, fraud, or unauthorized data access.
  • Accessing data belonging to another user.

Good-faith security reports should be sent privately to the contact address below. Do not publicly disclose exploitable details before a reasonable remediation period.

5. User content and project submissions

Submissions must not contain unlawful material, malware, stolen source code, exposed secrets, personal data posted without permission, or content that infringes copyright, trademark, privacy, or other rights.

Repository and live-project links must remain safe to open. FINISH may refuse to review links that trigger downloads, request credentials, contain deceptive redirects, or present security risks.

6. Academic integrity

FINISH encourages tools, collaboration, and AI-assisted learning where allowed, but you must not misrepresent another person's work as your own. When a final project or assessment requires original work, copying a solution without attribution may lead to rejection or loss of the completion record.

FINISH completion records are product records, not accredited degrees. You must not alter, forge, or represent them as credentials that FINISH did not issue.

7. Scraping and automation

Reasonable personal use is permitted. Without written permission, you may not:

  • Scrape paid assessments, private routes, learner data, or project reviews.
  • Bulk-download or republish FINISH-authored material.
  • Run bots that create accounts, submit quizzes, generate artificial progress, or overload the service.
  • Use automated means to bypass access controls or collect personal data.

Search-engine indexing of public pages and ordinary browser accessibility tools are not prohibited.

8. Harmful or unlawful conduct

Do not use FINISH to harass, threaten, impersonate, defraud, discriminate against, or unlawfully monitor another person. Do not use support channels for spam, extortion, or abusive communications.

9. Enforcement

Depending on severity, FINISH may warn the user, remove content, limit functionality, suspend access, terminate an account, revoke fraudulent completion records, preserve evidence, or report conduct to providers or authorities where required.

FINISH will consider context, intent, harm, history, and the possibility of correction. Immediate suspension may occur for active security threats, fraud, malware, or unlawful access.

Payment reversals caused by proven fraud may result in removal of the related course access. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.

10. Reporting and appeals

Report abuse or appeal an enforcement action

auroriumnexus@gmail.com

Include the account email, relevant URL or order, a factual description, and any supporting evidence. Do not send passwords or full financial credentials.
