PAYMENT POLICY

PayPal only. USD only.

FINISH uses one checkout provider and one course currency worldwide. This page explains how orders, captures, access, fees, refunds, and disputes work.

1. PayPal is the only payment provider

FINISH accepts course payments only through PayPal. Razorpay, Stripe, cryptocurrency, bank transfer, UPI, manual transaction references, and screenshot-based payments are not accepted by FINISH.

PayPal may offer PayPal balance, linked bank, card, or guest checkout options depending on your country and PayPal's own eligibility rules. FINISH does not control which funding methods PayPal presents.

2. One global price in USD

FINISH does not use IP address, country, browser language, timezone, or device location to change course prices.

The price displayed by FINISH is the USD price for the course. The same FINISH price applies globally. A future price change applies only to future purchases unless otherwise stated.

3. Checkout flow

  1. You sign in to a FINISH account.
  2. FINISH creates an internal order for the selected course and displayed USD amount.
  3. FINISH requests a corresponding PayPal order.
  4. You complete authorization on a PayPal-controlled page.
  5. PayPal returns the order for capture.
  6. FINISH verifies the capture server-side before granting access.

Closing or cancelling PayPal before capture does not create paid access.

4. Server-side verification

Course access is granted only after FINISH confirms the PayPal order, capture status, currency, amount, internal order reference, and user-course relationship. Duplicate events are handled idempotently so the same payment cannot grant duplicate access or duplicate accounting entries.

A screenshot, email receipt, bank message, PayPal notification, or transaction ID sent by a user is not enough by itself to unlock a course.

5. Currency conversion, fees, and taxes

PayPal, your card issuer, or your bank may convert USD into another currency and may apply exchange rates or fees. Those amounts are controlled by the relevant provider and are separate from the FINISH course price.

Applicable taxes may be included, added, withheld, or reported depending on law and PayPal's checkout configuration. The final PayPal approval screen is the authoritative total you authorize.

7. Failed, pending, or missing access

If PayPal shows a completed payment but FINISH does not grant access, do not pay again immediately. Email support with the account email, course, amount, date, and PayPal transaction identifier. FINISH will verify the capture and either restore access or handle the payment under the Refund Policy.

Pending payments do not grant access until PayPal and FINISH both confirm completion.

8. Fraud and risk controls

FINISH and PayPal may reject, delay, review, or reverse transactions associated with suspected fraud, sanctions restrictions, account compromise, mismatched order data, chargeback abuse, or automated attacks.

FINISH may suspend access while a payment is reversed or disputed. Legitimate users can contact support for review.

9. Refunds and reversals

Refund eligibility is governed by the Refund Policy and mandatory law. Approved refunds are sent to the original PayPal transaction. FINISH cannot redirect refunds to another PayPal account, bank account, wallet, or person.

If PayPal reverses or refunds a capture, the related course access may be removed unless FINISH determines that continued access is required.

10. Payment data and records

FINISH stores order and transaction metadata needed to grant access, prevent duplicates, issue refunds, resolve disputes, meet accounting obligations, and investigate abuse. FINISH does not store full card numbers, CVVs, bank passwords, or PayPal credentials. See the Privacy Policy.
