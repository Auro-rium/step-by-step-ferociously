Cookie and Storage Policy

FINISH uses necessary browser storage for accounts and course progress. Advertising and geographic-pricing trackers are not part of the product.

Effective: 2 August 2026No advertising pixelsNo location-pricing cookies

1. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small values stored by a browser and sent with later requests. Local storage and session storage keep information in the browser without attaching it to every request. Similar technologies may be used by embedded services to maintain sessions, remember preferences, prevent fraud, or measure service operation.

2. Technologies used directly by FINISH

TechnologyPurposeTypical duration
Supabase authentication storageKeeps you signed in, refreshes valid sessions, and associates requests with your account.Until expiry, sign-out, or browser deletion.
Application state and cacheImproves loading and preserves temporary interface state.Session or short-lived cache period.
Security and request metadataSupports fraud prevention, rate limiting, debugging, and abuse investigation.According to provider log-retention settings.

FINISH does not use browser storage to create regional prices or select a payment provider.

3. Third-party cookies and storage

YouTube / Google

Course videos are embedded through the YouTube player. YouTube may read or set cookies, local storage, identifiers, or interaction records when the player loads or you play a video. YouTube controls those technologies under its own policies.

PayPal

When you continue to PayPal, PayPal may use cookies, device identifiers, and fraud signals to authenticate you, remember checkout state, prevent abuse, comply with law, and process the payment.

Supabase and Vercel

These providers may use necessary infrastructure technologies and logs to deliver authentication, database, hosting, security, and network services.

4. Analytics and advertising

FINISH does not currently use third-party behavioral advertising pixels, cross-site retargeting tags, or advertising cookies. FINISH may use aggregated operational measurements such as page errors, request counts, or performance data to maintain the service.

If non-essential analytics are introduced later, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated before that use begins.

5. No location-based pricing storage

FINISH does not store a country, IP-derived region, browser language, or timezone for the purpose of changing course price. Every FINISH course uses its displayed global USD price and PayPal checkout.

Providers may independently process location-related signals for security, fraud checks, legal restrictions, and network delivery. FINISH does not turn those signals into a different course price.

6. Your browser controls

You can inspect, block, or delete cookies and site data through your browser settings. You can also sign out of FINISH to end the active account session.

  • Deleting FINISH authentication storage signs you out.
  • Blocking YouTube storage may prevent embedded videos from loading or remembering player state.
  • Blocking PayPal storage may prevent checkout or authentication from completing.
  • Private-browsing modes may clear sessions more aggressively.

7. Effects of disabling storage

Strictly necessary storage is required for account login, purchased access, secure checkout handoff, and some course functions. FINISH does not punish users for blocking non-essential technologies because the product does not currently depend on advertising trackers in the first place, a rare outbreak of restraint.

8. Changes

This policy will be updated when FINISH changes its browser storage, analytics, embedded services, or consent requirements. The effective date at the top will identify the current version.
