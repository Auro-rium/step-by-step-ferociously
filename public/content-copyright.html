1. The FINISH product model

FINISH selects educational playlists and builds an independent structured learning layer around them. Depending on the course, this may include ordering, checkpoints, quizzes, XP, progress records, project briefs, review workflows, and course-completion logic.

A FINISH purchase is for the FINISH structure and service. It is not a sale, sublicense, download, or transfer of ownership in the underlying source videos.

2. Third-party source videos and materials

Videos remain hosted by YouTube or another identified source platform. Copyright, trademarks, names, logos, lecture slides, and other source-course materials belong to their respective owners.

FINISH does not claim authorship of source lectures and does not intentionally reproduce complete source videos on FINISH servers. Embedding, linking, titles, thumbnails, and source attribution are used to identify and provide access to the source course within the structured route.

Third-party platforms and creators may restrict, remove, monetize, geoblock, or alter content. FINISH may update or remove affected routes when necessary.

3. FINISH-authored material

Unless otherwise stated, FINISH owns or licenses the original product interface, route design, course organization, quizzes, explanations, progress logic, XP systems, project briefs, policy text, branding, and original editorial material.

Your purchase gives you a limited, personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use that material within the purchased FINISH course. It does not permit commercial redistribution, resale, bulk copying, creation of a competing database, or publication of private assessments.

4. Source attribution and corrections

Course pages should identify the source title, creator or institution where available, and the original source URL. Attribution is descriptive and does not imply endorsement or partnership.

Creators and users may request correction of inaccurate title, creator, institution, link, or trademark information by contacting FINISH.

5. User project submissions

You retain ownership of original work you submit. By submitting project links, notes, or files, you grant FINISH a limited license to access, store, display, review, and process them only to provide the course, maintain records, resolve support matters, or enforce policies.

You must have permission to use code, images, datasets, music, trademarks, personal information, and other third-party material included in your submission. Open-source components remain governed by their licenses.

6. Prohibited copying and redistribution

Without written permission, users may not:

  • Publish or sell FINISH quiz banks, answer keys, project rubrics, or private route data.
  • Remove FINISH attribution from original product materials and present them as their own.
  • Use automated tools to copy paid FINISH content at scale.
  • Upload source videos or creator-owned materials to another platform in violation of their rights.
  • Use FINISH branding in a way that falsely implies sponsorship, certification, or partnership.

7. Copyright and trademark complaints

A rights holder or authorized representative may request review of allegedly infringing material by emailing the contact below. A complete notice should include:

  • Your name, organization, and contact information.
  • Identification of the protected work or mark.
  • The exact FINISH URL or material at issue.
  • An explanation of the claimed infringement and the authority under which you act.
  • A good-faith statement that the disputed use is not authorized by the rights holder, its agent, or law.
  • A statement that the information supplied is accurate.

FINISH may request reasonable verification before acting and may share the notice with the affected user or relevant provider when appropriate.

8. Source creator and institution requests

A verified creator, institution, or publisher may request corrected attribution, removal of a course route, replacement of an embed, or discussion of an official partnership. FINISH will review good-faith requests and may temporarily disable affected material during investigation.

Removing a source route does not transfer rights in FINISH-authored quizzes or product systems. FINISH will make reasonable efforts to protect existing purchasers through replacement content, route modification, or an appropriate remedy under the Refund Policy.

9. Repeat infringement and abuse

FINISH may remove submissions and suspend users who repeatedly infringe third-party rights or submit knowingly false infringement claims. Enforcement will consider the available evidence, context, and applicable law.
