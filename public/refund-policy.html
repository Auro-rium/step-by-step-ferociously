PURCHASE POLICY

Refund & Cancellation

FINISH courses are one-time digital purchases, not subscriptions. This policy explains when cancellation does not apply and the limited cases in which a payment may be corrected or refunded.

Effective from: 2 August 2026

1. Summary2. Cancellation3. Final sales4. Exceptions5. Not eligible6. Request process7. Provider rights8. Contact

1. Policy summary

One-time purchase. No recurring subscription. No ordinary cancellation after purchase.

Once payment is verified and digital course access is granted, the sale is final except for the limited exceptions below or where applicable law or a payment provider requires otherwise.

2. Cancellation is not applicable

FINISH does not charge a recurring subscription. Each checkout purchases permanent access to one FINISH course route. Because there is no future billing cycle to stop, subscription cancellation is not applicable.

You can stop using the service at any time, but non-use does not cancel or reverse a completed one-time purchase.

3. Digital access sales are final

After payment is verified and course access is made available to your account, ordinary refunds are not offered for change of mind, schedule changes, lack of use or dissatisfaction after access has begun.

Before paying, review the course title, source, lecture count, difficulty, inclusions, price and this policy. The checkout price is for the FINISH route, assessments, saved progress and capstone layer, not ownership of the public source lectures.

4. Limited exceptions

A correction or refund may be considered where:

  • the same transaction was charged more than once;
  • payment was captured but FINISH did not grant access, and support cannot restore access within a reasonable period;
  • the payment was unauthorised and validated as such by the payment provider or financial institution;
  • the wrong amount was captured because of a confirmed technical or pricing error;
  • a refund is required by applicable law, a court, card network, Razorpay, PayPal or another competent payment provider.

Where a technical access issue can be corrected promptly, restoration of access is the primary remedy.

5. Situations not ordinarily eligible

  • change of mind after purchase;
  • failure to start or complete the course;
  • purchasing the wrong course after the title and details were displayed;
  • disliking the teaching style or source lectures;
  • device, browser or internet limitations outside FINISH's control;
  • temporary source-video, YouTube or payment-provider outages;
  • failure to meet assessment or capstone requirements;
  • price reductions or promotions introduced after purchase.

6. How to report a payment problem

Email auroriumnexus@gmail.com with your account email, course name, payment date, amount, provider, order or payment identifier and a clear description of the issue.

Report duplicate charges or missing access promptly, preferably within 7 days, so transaction records and provider logs can be reviewed efficiently. This reporting period does not remove any mandatory right available under applicable law.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Processing time is controlled by the payment provider and financial institution.

7. Payment-provider and legal rights

This policy does not remove rights that cannot legally be excluded. PayPal Buyer Protection, card-network chargeback rights, bank dispute procedures and Razorpay dispute processes may apply independently.

Submitting a false dispute, claiming non-delivery after accessing the product or abusing chargebacks may result in account suspension and submission of access records to the payment provider, where lawful.

8. Contact

Purchase-policy questions can be sent to auroriumnexus@gmail.com.
