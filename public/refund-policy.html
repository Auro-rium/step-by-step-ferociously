PURCHASE POLICY

Refund Policy

FINISH courses are one-time digital purchases through PayPal. This policy defines a clear seven-day review window while protecting against completed-content abuse.

Effective: 2 August 2026Original payment method onlyNo subscription cancellation

1. Summary

You may request a refund within seven calendar days of payment when you have completed less than 20% of the course route and have not submitted the final assessment or project.

FINISH has no recurring subscription, so there is no future billing cycle to cancel. You can stop using the service at any time, but stopping use does not automatically reverse a completed payment.

2. Eligible refund requests

A refund may be approved when all ordinary eligibility conditions are met:

  • The request is received within seven calendar days of the PayPal payment.
  • Less than 20% of the course route is marked complete.
  • No cumulative final assessment or final project has been submitted.
  • The account is in good standing and the request is not connected to fraud, credential sharing, scraping, or policy abuse.

A refund may also be approved outside these ordinary conditions where required by law or where a confirmed payment or access failure falls under Section 4.

3. How usage is measured

FINISH may review server-side records including completed lessons, watch checkpoints, quiz attempts, project submissions, access timestamps, and payment events. Merely opening a course does not by itself eliminate refund eligibility.

The 20% threshold is based on the structured FINISH route, including required lessons and checkpoints available at the time of purchase.

4. Duplicate, unauthorized, or failed-access payments

FINISH will investigate and, where verified, correct or refund:

  • Duplicate captures for the same intended purchase.
  • A successful PayPal capture where course access was not granted and cannot be restored within a reasonable period.
  • An incorrect amount caused by a confirmed FINISH pricing or checkout error.
  • An unauthorized transaction confirmed through PayPal or the relevant financial institution.
  • A course that becomes materially inaccessible before meaningful use and cannot be repaired or reasonably replaced.

Where an access problem can be corrected promptly, restoring the purchased access is the primary remedy unless mandatory law requires a refund.

5. Requests not ordinarily eligible

  • Requests made more than seven days after payment without a legal or technical exception.
  • Completion of 20% or more of the route.
  • Submission of the final assessment or project.
  • Change of mind after substantial use.
  • Failure to begin or complete the course because of personal schedule changes.
  • Dislike of an instructor's teaching style when the source course was identified before purchase.
  • Device, browser, internet, or regional PayPal limitations outside FINISH's control.
  • Temporary YouTube, PayPal, Supabase, or Vercel outages that are resolved within a reasonable period.
  • Account suspension for fraud, infringement, security abuse, or another material policy breach.
  • Price reductions introduced after your purchase.

6. How to request a refund

Email auroriumnexus@gmail.com from the email linked to your FINISH account. Include:

  • Course title.
  • Payment date and amount.
  • PayPal order or transaction identifier.
  • Reason for the request.
  • Any screenshots relevant to a technical failure. Do not send card numbers, passwords, or unnecessary identity documents.

FINISH may ask for additional information reasonably necessary to verify the account or transaction.

7. Review and processing time

FINISH aims to review complete requests within five business days. Approved refunds are sent to the original PayPal funding method. PayPal, card issuers, and banks control final settlement timing, which may take additional business days.

FINISH cannot redirect a refund to another account, wallet, bank account, or payment method.

8. PayPal disputes and chargebacks

You may use PayPal's dispute process where available. Contacting FINISH first often resolves missing-access or duplicate-payment problems faster.

FINISH may provide PayPal with payment, login, progress, access, and support records relevant to a dispute. Knowingly filing a false non-delivery or unauthorized-use claim after using the product may result in account suspension, subject to applicable law.

9. Mandatory consumer rights

This policy does not remove any refund, cancellation, cooling-off, warranty, or dispute right that cannot legally be excluded in your location. Where mandatory law provides greater protection, that law controls.
