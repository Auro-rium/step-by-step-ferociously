PURCHASE POLICY

No-Refund Policy

FINISH courses are one-time digital purchases. After PayPal successfully captures a payment, the purchase is final and FINISH does not offer voluntary refunds.

Effective: 3 August 2026All sales finalNo subscription cancellation
Contents1. Summary2. Final-sale rule3. Review before purchase4. Access problems5. Payment corrections6. Disputes7. Mandatory rights8. Contact

1. Summary

All FINISH course purchases are final and non-refundable after successful PayPal capture.

FINISH sells one-time access to a digital learning route. There is no recurring subscription and no future billing cycle to cancel.

2. Final-sale rule

FINISH does not provide refunds, credits, exchanges, or transfers because of:

  • Change of mind.
  • Failure to begin or complete a course.
  • Personal schedule, device, browser, internet, or study-time limitations.
  • Dissatisfaction with the source instructor, teaching style, difficulty, assessment result, or final project.
  • Partial or substantial use of the course.
  • A later price reduction, promotion, or free-access offer.
  • Temporary outages or third-party interruptions that are restored within a reasonable period.
  • Account restriction caused by fraud, credential sharing, infringement, payment abuse, or another material policy breach.

Not using a purchased course does not create a refund or cancellation right.

3. Review before purchasing

Before paying, review the course title, description, source institution or educator, lesson count, difficulty, assessments, project requirements, price, and this policy. The checkout confirmation states that all sales are final.

Questions about whether a course is suitable should be sent to support before purchase.

4. Missing or defective access

If PayPal confirms a completed capture but FINISH does not unlock the course, contact support instead of paying again. FINISH will verify the transaction and restore the purchased access where possible.

Restoration, repair, replacement of an unavailable source lesson, or another reasonable method of supplying the purchased course is the primary remedy. A technical support request does not create a voluntary refund right.

5. Duplicate, unauthorized, or incorrect charges

FINISH will investigate a verified duplicate capture, unauthorized transaction, or incorrect amount. Where appropriate, FINISH or PayPal may correct, void, reverse, or return the affected transaction.

These payment corrections are not a general refund programme and do not apply to change of mind, non-use, dissatisfaction, or course performance.

Send only the course title, FINISH account email, payment date, amount, and PayPal order or transaction identifier. Never send passwords, full card numbers, CVVs, or unnecessary identity documents.

6. PayPal disputes and chargebacks

PayPal and card issuers may provide dispute rights under their own rules. FINISH may provide relevant payment, account, access, progress, assessment, project, and support records when responding to a dispute.

Knowingly filing a false non-delivery or unauthorized-use claim after accessing the product may result in account restriction, subject to applicable law.

7. Mandatory consumer rights

This policy does not exclude any remedy, reversal, warranty, dispute right, or consumer protection that applicable law or binding payment-network rules do not allow FINISH to exclude. Where such rules require a different result, those rules control.
