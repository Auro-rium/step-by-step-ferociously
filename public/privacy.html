Privacy Policy

This policy explains what FINISH collects, why it is used, which service providers receive it, and the choices available to you.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the FINISH website, account system, course catalog, checkout flow, learning routes, quizzes, XP, progress tracking, final project submissions, and support communications.

FINISH is a structured learning product built around selected third-party educational videos. PayPal, YouTube, Supabase, and Vercel operate under their own privacy terms when you interact with their services.

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Encrypted authentication credentials and account identifiers managed through Supabase Auth.
  • Account role, such as learner or administrator.

Learning activity

  • Courses purchased or granted.
  • Lesson progress, completion state, and watch-percentage checkpoints.
  • Quiz attempts, answers, scores, pass status, XP, and streaks.
  • Final project links, notes, submission status, and administrator feedback.

Payment records

  • Course, amount, currency, order status, timestamps, and internal order identifiers.
  • PayPal order, capture, refund, and dispute identifiers needed to verify access and resolve payment issues.
  • FINISH does not receive or store your full card number, bank credentials, or PayPal password.

Technical and support information

  • Browser and device information, request logs, error logs, security events, and network metadata that may be automatically processed by hosting, database, video, and payment providers.
  • Messages and attachments you send to support.

3. How we use information

  • Create and secure accounts.
  • Verify purchases and grant course access.
  • Save progress and determine when lessons, quizzes, or final projects unlock.
  • Review final project submissions and provide feedback.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and duplicate XP or payment events.
  • Diagnose failures, maintain availability, and improve the product.
  • Respond to support, refund, privacy, and legal requests.
  • Comply with applicable law and enforce FINISH policies.

FINISH does not sell personal information and does not use personal information for third-party advertising.

4. No IP-based or location-based pricing

FINISH uses one global PayPal price in USD. FINISH does not use your IP address, browser locale, device location, or country to set a different course price or choose a different payment provider.

Infrastructure and payment providers may still process IP addresses or approximate location for network delivery, security, fraud prevention, sanctions screening, legal compliance, or their own service operation. That processing is not used by FINISH to alter the course price.

6. Cookies, local storage, and similar technologies

FINISH uses browser storage and strictly necessary technologies to keep you signed in, maintain secure sessions, remember product state, and operate purchased learning routes. FINISH does not currently deploy advertising pixels or location-pricing cookies.

Embedded YouTube content and PayPal checkout may set or read their own cookies or storage. Details appear in the Cookie and Storage Policy.

7. Retention

  • Account and learning records are generally kept while your account remains active so purchased access and progress can be restored.
  • Payment records may be retained as required for accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, and legal compliance.
  • Support records are retained for as long as needed to resolve the request and maintain an appropriate audit trail.
  • Deleted data may remain temporarily in backups or logs until routine deletion cycles complete.

Where possible, FINISH will delete or anonymize data that is no longer reasonably needed.

8. Security

FINISH uses access controls, server-side payment verification, row-level database permissions, encrypted transport, provider-managed authentication, and restricted administrator functions. No internet service can promise absolute security. You are responsible for using a strong password and protecting your account session.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection regarding your personal information. You may also ask for an explanation of a payment or account decision.

Send requests to auroriumnexus@gmail.com from the email associated with your account. FINISH may need to verify your identity before acting. Some data may be retained when required for payments, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or legal claims.

10. Children and students

FINISH is not directed to children under 13. Users who are below the age at which they can independently enter contracts in their country should use FINISH only with permission from a parent or legal guardian. FINISH does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

11. International processing

FINISH and its providers may process information in countries other than your own. Privacy and data-protection laws may differ across locations. FINISH relies on provider safeguards and applicable legal mechanisms where required.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the product, providers, or legal requirements change. Material changes will be identified by a new effective date and, where appropriate, an in-product notice.
