Privacy Policy

This policy explains what FINISH collects, why it is used, where it is processed and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 2 August 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the FINISH website, account system, course catalog, course player, assessments, capstone submissions, support interactions and payment-related records.

Third-party source videos and payment pages have their own privacy practices. FINISH does not control those external services.

2. Information collected

Account information

Email address, display name, account identifier, authentication metadata and role.

Learning activity

Course enrollments, lecture progress, watch completion, quiz answers and scores, XP, streaks, capstone submissions, repository links, live-project links and review status.

Purchase and support records

Course purchased, price, currency, payment provider, provider transaction identifiers, order status, timestamps and messages you send to support.

Technical and regional information

Approximate country or region used to select INR or international pricing, browser and device information, IP-derived request metadata, application logs, error data and security events.

FINISH does not intentionally collect card numbers, UPI PINs, CVVs or PayPal passwords. Those are handled by the relevant payment provider.

3. How information is used

  • create and secure your account;
  • remember course progress and determine the next unlocked step;
  • score assessments and maintain completion records;
  • process and verify purchases;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, duplicate unlocks and unauthorised access;
  • respond to support and privacy requests;
  • maintain, debug and improve product reliability;
  • comply with legal, accounting and payment-provider obligations.

FINISH does not sell personal data to advertisers.

4. Payment information

Indian checkout is processed through Razorpay and international checkout through PayPal. FINISH receives transaction metadata needed to verify payment and grant access, but payment credentials are entered directly into provider-controlled interfaces.

Payment providers may independently process identity, fraud, device and financial information under their own policies. Their dispute and buyer-protection processes may apply even where FINISH's ordinary refund policy does not.

5. Service providers and disclosures

Information may be processed by vendors that operate the product, including:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, storage and server-side functions;
  • Vercel for website hosting, delivery and runtime logs;
  • YouTube and Google for embedded source lectures;
  • Razorpay for Indian payments;
  • PayPal for international payments.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect users or the service, to investigate fraud, or as part of a legitimate business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

6. Cookies and local storage

FINISH uses browser storage and authentication cookies necessary to keep you signed in, preserve sessions and operate course progress. Embedded YouTube content and payment providers may set their own cookies or similar technologies.

FINISH does not currently use third-party behavioural advertising cookies.

7. Data retention

Account and learning data is retained while your account remains active and for a reasonable period afterwards to preserve purchased access, meet legal obligations, prevent fraud and resolve disputes.

Payment and accounting records may be retained for the period required by law and payment providers. Security logs are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for reliability and abuse prevention.

8. Security

FINISH uses managed authentication, access controls, server-side payment verification and restricted credentials. No system can guarantee absolute security. You should use a unique password and notify support promptly if you suspect account compromise.

9. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or a copy of your personal information, and may object to or restrict certain processing.

Some data cannot be deleted immediately where it is required to preserve paid access, comply with legal obligations, investigate abuse or resolve payment disputes. Requests will be verified before action is taken.

To request account deletion or a data copy, email the contact address below from the email associated with your account.

10. International processing

FINISH and its service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Those locations may have different data-protection laws. Service providers are selected to operate the product securely and are subject to their own contractual and legal obligations.

11. Children and student users

FINISH is not directed to children under 13. Users who are below the age at which they can independently enter a contract in their location should use FINISH only with permission from a parent or legal guardian.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the product, vendors or legal requirements change. The updated date will appear at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated through the product or account email where reasonably possible.

13. Privacy contact

Privacy and data requests can be sent to auroriumnexus@gmail.com. Include the email associated with your FINISH account and enough information to verify and process the request.
